### 풀이
```python
TC = int(input())
for tc in range(1, TC+1):
    N, K = map(int, input().split())
    dp = [0]*(K+1)
    dp[0] = 1
    
    for i in range(1,N+1):
        for j in range(K, i-1, -1):
            dp[j] += dp[j-i]
    
    print(f'#{tc} {dp[K]}')
```
